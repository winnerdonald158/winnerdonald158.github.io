Project Overview

My computer had been consuming a significant amount of internet data. I used Windows SRUM records, PostgreSQL, and Power BI to investigate which processes were associated with network traffic, how much data was received and sent, and when network activity was recorded most often.

I organized the analysis around five business questions, using SQL to examine traffic by process, direction, hour, and month. I then used Power BI to present the findings in a business-focused dashboard.

Goal

To understand the main sources and patterns of recorded network traffic, identify processes associated with higher usage, and highlight areas that require further investigation.

Process

  • I extracted Windows SRUM data using SrumECmd.
  • I prepared the network usage data for analysis.
  • I loaded the data into PostgreSQL and used SQL to investigate total traffic, received data, sent data, and time-based patterns.
  • I used process mapping to make executable paths easier to interpret.
  • I distinguished records with genuinely blank executable information from records that did not match the process-mapping table.
  • I used the findings to build a Power BI dashboard and identify priorities for further investigation.

Key Insights

  • 159.44 GB of total network traffic was recorded. The dashboard showed 134.99 GB received and 24.44 GB sent.
  • 79.81 GB had no recorded executable name. This made unidentified network activity the main priority for further investigation.
  • Google Chrome was a major identifiable contributor. It accounted for approximately 39.23 GB received and 8.65 GB sent.
  • Early-morning activity stood out. The hourly record-count chart showed its highest displayed count at 4 AM, with 1,857 records.
  • Unidentified activity and Google Chrome showed significant activity over time. Their patterns were examined across July, August, and September 2026.

Investigation Priorities

  • Investigate network records without a recorded executable name.
  • Compare early-morning network records with available application timeline and resource information.
  • Review the contribution of browser and VPN-related processes.
  • Continue monitoring traffic volume and record frequency over time.
